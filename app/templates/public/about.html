Who We Are • RC: 7592601

About Fact Box Consult

We tell stories that inspire action, amplify impact, and drive meaningful change.

Fact Box Consult (RC: 7592601) is an audiovisual media, research, and creative consultancy committed to developing fresh ideas and transforming them into meaningful content and solutions.

Our passion for excellence drives every project we undertake. We combine creativity, research, and strategic thinking to deliver content and services that are relevant, engaging, accurate, and purposeful.

Operating within a multicultural environment, we bring diverse perspectives to our work, enabling us to create content that connects with different audiences and reflects real experiences. Our strength lies in our ability to combine audiovisual storytelling, research, digital media, and strategic communication to meet the evolving needs of individuals, organizations, development partners, and businesses.

Vision Statement

To create credible, timely, and impactful content grounded in accurate information, strong research, creativity, and real-world relevance.

We envision a media and research organization recognised for producing content that people can trust, use, and connect with.

Over the years, our team has developed experience across digital and new media, research, radio, television production, media consultancy, and strategic communication. Our professionals have worked both independently and collaboratively with local and international organizations, giving Fact Box Consult the capacity to manage projects professionally from concept development through production and final delivery.

We are committed to building Fact Box Consult as a leading integrated content and research hub, creating work that informs, inspires, and delivers measurable value to our clients and audiences.

Our Approach

At Fact Box Consult, we believe that good content should do more than look professional—it should tell a story, communicate a clear message, and create value. We therefore combine creativity with research and strategic thinking to ensure that every project is purposeful, audience-focused, and delivered to a high professional standard.
