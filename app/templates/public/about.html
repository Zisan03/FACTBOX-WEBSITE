About Us.

verified Audiovisual Media • Research • Consultancy

Stories That Connect. Messages That Matter. Grounded in Truth & Research.

Fact Box Consult (RC: 7592601) is an audiovisual media, research, and creative consultancy committed to developing fresh ideas and transforming them into meaningful content and solutions.

Operating within a multicultural environment, we bring diverse perspectives to our work, enabling us to create content that connects with different audiences and reflects authentic human experiences.

Vision Statement

To create credible, timely, and impactful content grounded in accurate information, strong research, creativity, and real-world relevance.

We envision a media and research organization recognised for producing content that people can trust, use, and connect with.

Our passion for excellence drives every project we undertake. We combine creativity, research, and strategic thinking to deliver content and services that are relevant, engaging, accurate, and purposeful.

“Our strength lies in our ability to combine audiovisual storytelling, research, digital media, and strategic communication to meet the evolving needs of individuals, organizations, development partners, and businesses.”

Mission Mandate

Making communication simple, stories meaningful, and information accessible to all.

Core Principles

Communicating With People, Not Talking At Them

It is about creating messages that are clear, relatable, inclusive, and meaningful, regardless of a person’s educational level, language, culture, or social background:

Empathy

Understanding the people receiving the message and considering their experiences.

Clarity

Using simple, familiar language that people can easily understand.

Inclusivity

Communicating in ways that respect different backgrounds, perspectives, and abilities.

Respect

Sharing information without demeaning, misleading, or unnecessarily excluding anyone.

Impact

Ensuring communication builds understanding, strengthens relationships, and encourages positive action.
