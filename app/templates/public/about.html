About Fact Box Consult

Fact Box Consult (RC: 7592601) is an audiovisual media, research, and creative consultancy committed to developing fresh ideas and transforming them into meaningful content and solutions.

Operating within a multicultural environment, we bring diverse perspectives to our work, enabling us to create content that connects with different audiences and reflects real experiences.

Our Vision

To create credible, timely, and impactful content grounded in accurate information, strong research, creativity, and real-world relevance. We envision a media and research organization recognized for producing content that people can trust, use, and connect with.

At Fact Box Consult, we believe that good content should do more than look professional—it should tell a story, communicate a clear message, and create value.
